Delivery details, kept simple.

Where does Komorebi Kaffee deliver?

We deliver to serviceable addresses in Noida from our kitchen in Sector 70. Enter your delivery address in the Ownly app to check.

Do you use premix?

We brew with 100% Arabica coffee and use no premix or instant coffee.

How can I order?

Open the Ownly app on your phone to order. For nearby, repeat, or bulk orders, message us on WhatsApp.

What should I order first?

If you like creamy coffee, start with the Iced Latte. If you like strong and sweet, try the Vietnamese Cold Coffee. If you prefer black coffee, go with the Iced Americano.
